#title:高中英文二1-R1s Part 1 (Unit 1 & 2)
champion|noun|冠軍|Howard will be crowned the world chess champion after winning the final next Monday.|在下星期一贏得決賽後，Howard將會被冠上西洋棋世界冠軍的頭銜。
dedication|noun|奉獻；投入|Doctor Parker shows total dedication to this project. She has spent a lot of time doing detailed studies since she began the project two years ago.|Parker博士完全投入在這個計畫中。自從兩年前她開始這個計畫，她已經花了很多時間做詳盡的研究。
medal|noun|獎牌|Liz dreamed of coming in first in the race and wearing a shiny gold medal around her neck.|Liz夢想在賽跑中得到第一名，並戴著一枚閃亮的金牌在她脖子上。
injury|noun|受傷|After suffering a serious injury in her last race, the cyclist has no chance of becoming a world champion this time.|在經歷上一場比賽嚴重的受傷之後，這名自行車選手這一次就沒有機會成為世界冠軍了。
injury|noun|受傷|Aunt May crashed her car into a tree last night. Fortunately, she escaped without injury.|May阿姨昨晚開車撞到樹。幸好她毫髮無傷地逃過一劫。
success|noun|成功|Monica had great success in her work and became a famous singer after years of dedication.|經過多年的投入，Monica在她的工作上獲得巨大成功，並且成為一位知名的歌手。
pain|noun|疼痛|Alex experienced a sharp pain in his leg while hiking.|在健行的時候，Alex感受到腿部一陣刺痛。
pain|noun|疼痛|Once the dentist pulled out Alice's rotten tooth, she no longer suffered from the pain.|一旦牙醫將Alice的蛀牙拔掉，她就不再遭受疼痛了。
immediately|adv.|立即；馬上|I need to leave immediately, or I'll miss my plane.|我必須要馬上離開，否則我會錯過我的飛機。
collapse|verb|倒下|The woman felt dizzy after the seven-hour train journey and suddenly collapsed in the station.|在七個小時的火車旅行後，那名婦人感到頭暈，並且突然在車站裡倒下。
medical|adj.|醫療的|Lisa plans to attend medical school and become a doctor in the future.|Lisa計畫就讀醫學院，並且在未來成為一位醫生。
staff|noun|全體員工|The hotel staff greets all guests in a polite way.|那家飯店的員工禮貌地向所有客人打招呼。
staff|noun|全體員工|The movie theater has a small staff of fifteen part-time workers.|這家電影院只有少少的十五名兼職員工。
courage|noun|勇氣|It takes courage for first-time swimmers to put their heads under the water.|對於第一次游泳的人而言，將頭放進水下需要勇氣。
strength|noun|意志力|It's important for a player to have enough mental strength to handle the stress during competitions.|一名選手有足夠的意志力去處理比賽時的壓力是重要的。
support|noun|支持|The crowd cheered loudly to show their support for the tennis player every time she won a point.|每當她贏得一分，群眾就會大聲歡呼來表現出他們對於這位網球選手的支持。
struggle|verb|艱難地前進|With a heavy load in back, the truck struggled slowly up the steep hill.|後面載著重物，那輛卡車艱難地慢慢往陡峭的山上開去。
pride|noun|驕傲；得意|Neil took pride in his brother's determination when he saw him complete the marathon.|在看到他的弟弟完成馬拉松賽跑時，Neil對他的決心感到驕傲。
emotional|adj.|情緒激動的|Mr. Jones got very emotional and struggled to hold back the tears when his daughter graduated from law school.|當Jones先生的女兒從法學院畢業的時候，他變得很激動，而且努力地要忍住淚水。
admiration|noun|欽佩|Fans showed their admiration for the amazing actor by throwing flowers up onto the stage after the show.|在表演之後，粉絲們將花丟到臺上以展現他們對這位出色演員的欽佩。
respect|noun|尊敬；尊重|Mike had great respect for the wise old man and always treated him politely.|Mike非常尊敬這位有智慧的老先生，並且總是禮貌地對待他。
take off|phrase|出發|As soon as these firefighters were ready, they took off at great speed toward the burning building.|這些消防隊員一就緒，他們就以極快速度出發前往那棟失火的建築物。
take off|phrase|起飛|After racing down the runway, the large plane successfully took off into the air.|在跑道上衝刺後，這架大型飛機成功地起飛至空中。
have one's heart set on|phrase|一心一意想要做某事|Toby has his heart set on being an NBA player and trains for hours every day to improve his basketball skills.|Toby一心一意想要當一名NBA球員，而且每天訓練好幾個小時來增進他的球技。
speed (...) up|phrase|加速|We are behind schedule, so we need to speed up and work more quickly.|我們進度落後了，所以我們需要加快速度，工作更快一些。
rise to one's feet|phrase|（重新）站起來|When the injured boxer rose to his feet after being knocked down, everyone clapped wildly.|當這位受傷的拳擊手在被擊倒後重新站了起來時，每個人都熱烈地鼓掌。
carry...through|phrase|幫助……渡過難關|It was Evan's support that carried Mandy through when she lost her sister in a car accident.|當Mandy在一場車禍中失去她的姊姊時，是Evan的支持幫助她渡過難關。
warn|verb|警告；提醒|Bill's parents warned him not to go mountain climbing because a strong typhoon was coming.|Bill的父母警告他不要去爬山，因為一個強烈颱風即將到來。
announcement|noun|公告；廣播通知|Our English teacher made an announcement that there would be a test on Thursday.|我們的英文老師公告星期四將有一個測驗。
research|noun|研究|Dr. Smith is doing some research into how dolphins communicate with each other.|Smith博士正在研究海豚是如何彼此溝通的。
flavor|noun|味道|The soup has no flavor. You should add some salt to it.|這個湯沒有味道。你應該加一些鹽進去。
flavor|noun|味道|The tea that is grown in this mountain village has a gentle flavor of flowers.|這個山中村落所種植的茶葉有一種淡淡的花香味。
pressure|noun|（液體、氣體等）壓力|Doctors often warn patients with high blood pressure to avoid high-fat diets.|醫生們常常警告有高血壓的病人避免高油脂的飲食。
normally|adv.|通常|Uncle Frank normally reads the newspaper over breakfast, but this morning he watched the news on TV instead.|Frank叔叔通常在吃早餐的時候讀報紙，但是今天早上他改看電視新聞。
distinguish|verb|分辨|Nancy and her twin sister look so similar that even their parents can't distinguish between them sometimes.|Nancy跟她的雙胞胎妹妹看起來如此像，以致於連她們的父母有時候也無法分辨她們。
distinguish|verb|分辨|As an art expert, Victor can easily distinguish a real painting from a copy.|身為一位藝術專家，Victor能輕易地分辨真畫與複製品。
accurately|adv.|精確地；準確地|So far scientists have not found a way to predict earthquakes accurately.|目前為止，科學家還沒有找到精確預測地震的方法。
function|verb|運作；起作用|Malcom's favorite couch also functions as a bed when one of his friends stays over for the night.|當Malcom的一位朋友留下來過夜時，他最愛的沙發也可以當床用。
blame|verb|歸咎於；責怪|The police blamed the drunken driver for the terrible car accident.|警方將這場可怕的車禍歸咎於那名酒醉的駕駛。
affect|verb|影響|Global warming has greatly affected the lives of many wild animals, such as polar bears.|全球暖化已經嚴重影響到許多野生動物的生活，像是北極熊。
passenger|noun|乘客|Passengers on the plane were glad to hear the captain announce that the strong winds wouldn't affect their flight.|機上的乘客很高興聽到機長宣布說強勁的風勢不會影響到他們的航班。
distract|verb|使分心；使轉移注意力|Ken tried to finish the novel before bed, but the loud music from next door kept distracting him from his reading.|Ken試著要在睡前讀完小說，但隔壁吵雜的音樂聲讓他一直無法專心閱讀。
indicate|verb|指出|Recent research clearly indicates that only 3% of the children in this country get as much daily exercise as they should.|最近的研究清楚地指出這個國家只有百分之三的小孩做足每天該做的運動。
conditions|noun|情況；環境|Under normal conditions, my scooter functions well, but yesterday it was so cold that it wouldn't start.|在正常的情況下，我的機車運作地很好，但昨天太冷了以致於它發不動。
solution|noun|解決方法|The government is trying to think of a solution to the problem of air pollution.|政府正在試著想出一個解決空氣汙染的方法。
ingredient|noun|（食物的）材料；成分|This restaurant only uses natural ingredients like fruits, vegetables, and herbs in its dishes.|這家餐廳只使用天然的食材像是水果、蔬菜和食用香草在它的菜餚裡。
recipe|noun|食譜|Does this cookbook include a recipe for apple pie?|這本料理書包含蘋果派的食譜嗎？
comparison|noun|比較|The cost of living in Taipei is quite high. By comparison, it doesn't cost so much to live in any other city in Taiwan.|臺北的生活費用相當高。相比之下，住在臺灣任何其他城市的費用就沒有這麼高。
comparison|noun|比較|In her latest book, the travel writer made a comparison of the eating habits in different countries.|在她最新的書當中，這位旅遊作家將不同國家的飲食習慣做了比較。
remain|verb|保持|When Judy went back to her hometown twenty years later, she found that most things remained unchanged.|Judy在二十年後回到她的家鄉時，她發現大部分的東西都保持不變。
provide|verb|提供|This hotel provides a nice breakfast and free Wi-Fi for its guests.|這間飯店提供房客美好的早餐以及免費的無線網路。
pleasant|adj.|令人愉快的|On summer days, it is pleasant to go for a walk in the evening when it is cooler.|夏天的時候，在傍晚較涼爽時去散步是很令人愉快的。
be used to|phrase|習慣|Arnold is already used to the weather on the island, so the heat and strong winds don't bother him at all.|Arnold已經習慣這島上的天氣，所以炎熱和強風一點都不會困擾他。
needless to say|phrase|不用說|Miranda performed better than all her co-workers last year. Needless to say, she got a pay raise and a promotion.|Miranda去年的表現比她所有的同事好。不用說，她加薪又升官。
believe it or not|phrase|信不信由你|Believe it or not, you have just won one million dollars in our lottery!|信不信由你，你剛從我們的樂透彩贏得一百萬元！
come up with|phrase|想出|We came up with a great plan to hold a surprise party for Gina on her birthday.|我們想到了一個很棒的計畫來為Gina的生日舉辦一場驚喜派對。
in comparison with|phrase|和……相較之下|In comparison with Greenland, Taiwan is a very small island.|和格陵蘭相較之下，臺灣是一座非常小的島嶼。
regardless of|phrase|不管|The food festival will be held as planned regardless of the weather.|無論天氣如何，這場美食嘉年華將如期舉行。
