#title:中級學術字彙 L1-L2
approach|noun|方式、方法|We need a new approach to solve this problem.|我們需要一個新方法來解決這個問題。
approach|verb|接近；接洽；著手處理|Winter is approaching and it's getting colder.|冬天正在逼近，天氣越來越冷。
authority|noun|權力、職權；官方、當權者|The local authority approved the construction plan.|地方當局批准了建設計畫。
beneficial|adj.|有利的、有助的|Regular exercise is beneficial to your health.|規律運動對你的健康有益。
benefit|noun/verb|利益、好處；對...有幫助、使受惠|One benefit of this job is the flexible schedule.|這份工作的一個好處是彈性的時間。
concept|noun|概念|The concept of democracy is widely discussed.|民主的概念被廣泛討論。
consist|verb|在於、由…組成|The team consists of ten members.|這個團隊由十名成員組成。
constitute|verb|組成、構成；被視為；(合法或正式地)設立、成立|Women constitute 60% of the workforce.|女性佔勞動力的百分之六十。
context|noun|語境、上下文、(事情發生的)背景|You need to understand the context of the conversation.|你需要了解對話的背景。
define|verb|下定義、規定|How would you define success?|你會如何定義成功？
distribute|verb|分配、發送|Volunteers distributed food to the homeless.|志工們將食物分發給無家可歸的人。
economic|adj.|經濟的|The country is facing an economic crisis.|該國正面臨經濟危機。
economy|noun|經濟|The global economy has been affected by the pandemic.|全球經濟受到了疫情的影響。
environmental|adj.|環境的|Environmental pollution is a serious global issue.|環境污染是一個嚴重的全球議題。
establish|verb|建立|The company was established in 1990.|這家公司成立於一九九零年。
estimate|verb/noun|估計、估算|We estimate the cost to be around $5,000.|我們估計費用約為五千美元。
evidence|noun/verb|證據；作為證據、證明|There is strong evidence to support the theory.|有強有力的證據支持這個理論。
evident|adj.|顯而易見的|It is evident that she has talent for music.|顯然她在音樂方面很有天賦。
factor|noun|因素|Price is an important factor in consumer decisions.|價格是消費者決策的重要因素。
finance|noun/verb|金融、財政；(提供)資金|The bank agreed to finance the construction project.|銀行同意資助這個建設專案。
formula|noun|公式、方程式|The scientist discovered a new formula for the drug.|科學家發現了這種藥物的新配方。
functional|adj.|實用的、功能的|The elevator is not functional right now.|電梯目前無法使用。
identify|verb|確認、認出|Can you identify the suspect in this photo?|你能辨認出照片中的嫌疑犯嗎？
interpret|verb|詮釋、理解為|It's difficult to interpret his silence.|很難解讀他的沉默。
interpretation|noun|詮釋、理解|There are many interpretations of this poem.|這首詩有許多不同的詮釋。
involve|verb|牽涉、包含、參加|The project involves a lot of teamwork.|這個專案涉及大量的團隊合作。
issue|noun|議題、問題|Climate change is a major issue worldwide.|氣候變遷是全球的重大議題。
issue|verb|發行|The government issued a warning about the typhoon.|政府發布了颱風警報。
labor|noun/verb|勞動、勞工；努力做 = labour (British English)|The workers demanded better labor conditions.|工人們要求更好的勞動條件。
percent|noun|百分比、百分之一 = per cent|Over 70 percent of students passed the exam.|超過百分之七十的學生通過了考試。
percentage|noun|百分率|What percentage of the budget goes to education?|預算中有多少百分比用於教育？
proceed|verb|繼續進行、前進|Please proceed to the next chapter.|請繼續進行下一章。
process|noun/verb|過程；處理、加工|Learning a language is a long process.|學習一門語言是一個漫長的過程。
research|noun/verb|研究、調查|The research shows that exercise improves memory.|研究顯示運動可以改善記憶力。
respond|verb|回應、回答|She responded to the email within an hour.|她在一小時內回覆了電子郵件。
sector|noun|部門、分區|The technology sector is growing rapidly.|科技產業正在快速成長。
significant|adj.|顯著的、有重大意義的|There has been a significant improvement in his grades.|他的成績有了顯著的進步。
specific|adj.|特定的、明確的|Can you give me a more specific example?|你能給我一個更具體的例子嗎？
structure|noun/verb|結構、建築物；構成、組織|The structure of the building is very strong.|這棟建築的結構非常堅固。
theory|noun|學說、理論、看法|Einstein's theory of relativity is well known.|愛因斯坦的相對論廣為人知。
vary|verb|不同、相異|Prices vary depending on the season.|價格依季節而有所不同。
abnormal|adj.|不正常的|The doctor noticed abnormal results in the blood test.|醫生注意到血液檢查有異常結果。
acquire|verb|獲得、購得|She acquired a new skill through online courses.|她透過線上課程習得了一項新技能。
appropriate|adj.|合適、恰當的|Please wear appropriate clothing for the occasion.|請穿著適合該場合的服裝。
aspect|noun|方面、層面|We must consider every aspect of the issue.|我們必須考慮這個問題的每個面向。
assist|verb|協助、援助|A nurse assisted the doctor during the surgery.|一位護士在手術中協助醫生。
assistance|noun|幫忙、援助|Thank you for your assistance with the project.|感謝您對這個專案的幫助。
commission|noun/verb|委任、委員會；委託|The government set up a commission to investigate.|政府成立了一個委員會進行調查。
commission|noun|佣金、手續費|He earns a 10% commission on each sale.|他每筆銷售可獲得百分之十的佣金。
community|noun|社區、團體|The local community organized a charity event.|當地社區舉辦了一場慈善活動。
complex|adj./noun|複雜的、難懂的；複合物、建築群、綜合大樓|The human brain is a complex organ.|人類的大腦是一個複雜的器官。
compute|verb|計算|The software can compute the results in seconds.|這個軟體可以在幾秒內計算出結果。
conclude|verb|作出結論、斷定、結束|The meeting concluded at 5 p.m.|會議在下午五點結束。
conduct|verb|執行、引導、指揮(合唱團或樂隊)；表現、舉止|The scientist conducted an experiment in the lab.|科學家在實驗室進行了一項實驗。
conduct|noun|行為、舉止|His conduct at school has improved greatly.|他在學校的行為大幅改善。
consequence|noun|結果、後果|He suffered the consequences of his bad decisions.|他承受了錯誤決定的後果。
consequent|adj.|隨之發生的|The storm and the consequent flooding caused great damage.|暴風雨及隨之而來的洪水造成了巨大損害。
construct|verb/noun|建造、組成；概念、構想|They plan to construct a new hospital.|他們計畫建造一家新醫院。
consume|verb|消耗、花費；(大量地)吃、喝|Americans consume a lot of fast food.|美國人消耗大量速食。
credit|noun|讚許；(經濟上的)信用、賒帳、信用貸款；學分|He deserves credit for the success of the project.|這個專案的成功應歸功於他。
credit|verb|把錢存進帳戶；歸功於|The bank credited the money to my account.|銀行把錢存進了我的帳戶。
cultural|adj.|文化的|Cultural differences can sometimes cause misunderstandings.|文化差異有時會造成誤解。
distinct|adj.|不同的、明顯的|The two species are quite distinct from each other.|這兩個物種彼此截然不同。
distinction|noun|差別；優秀|She graduated with distinction from the university.|她以優異成績從大學畢業。
evaluate|verb|評估、評價|The teacher evaluates the students' progress every month.|老師每月評估學生的進步情況。
feature|noun/verb|特色；以…為特色|The phone has many useful features.|這支手機有很多實用的功能。
impact|noun/verb|衝擊；對…產生影響|Social media has a huge impact on young people.|社群媒體對年輕人有巨大的影響。
injure|verb|受傷、傷害|He injured his knee while playing basketball.|他打籃球時傷了膝蓋。
institute|noun|學院、協會|She works at a research institute.|她在一間研究機構工作。
invest|verb|投資、投入時間|She decided to invest her savings in stocks.|她決定將存款投資於股票。
journal|noun|報刊雜誌、日記|He published his findings in a scientific journal.|他在科學期刊上發表了他的研究發現。
normal|adj.|正常的、標準的|Everything returned to normal after the storm.|暴風雨過後一切恢復正常。
obtain|verb|獲得|You can obtain a visa at the embassy.|你可以在大使館取得簽證。
participant|noun|參與者、參加者|All participants must register before the event.|所有參加者必須在活動前報名。
participate|verb|參與、參加|Students are encouraged to participate in class discussions.|鼓勵學生參與課堂討論。
potential|adj./noun|潛在的、可能的；潛力、可能性|She has the potential to become a great leader.|她有成為傑出領導者的潛力。
previous|adj.|先前的|She had no previous experience in teaching.|她之前沒有教學經驗。
primary|noun|(美國推舉黨內候選人的)初選|The candidate won the primary election easily.|候選人輕鬆贏得了初選。
regional|adj.|地方的、區域的|Regional foods vary greatly across the country.|各地區的食物差異很大。
regulate|verb|規範、調節|The government regulates food safety standards.|政府管控食品安全標準。
resource|noun|資源|Water is a precious natural resource.|水是珍貴的自然資源。
restrict|verb|限制、束縛|The government restricted the use of plastic bags.|政府限制了塑膠袋的使用。
secure|verb/adj.|保護、使安全；安心的、安全的|Make sure to secure all the windows before you leave.|離開前請確保所有窗戶都鎖好。
site|noun|位址、網站|The construction site is closed on weekends.|工地在週末關閉。
strategy|noun|策略、戰略|We need a new strategy to increase sales.|我們需要一個新策略來增加銷售。
survey|noun/verb|(民意)調查；查看、審視|The company conducted a survey on customer satisfaction.|公司進行了一項顧客滿意度調查。
text|noun|正文、文本|The teacher asked us to read the text carefully.|老師要我們仔細閱讀課文。
transfer|noun/verb|調動、搬遷|He transferred to a new school last year.|他去年轉到了一所新學校。
