#title:高中英文二1-R1s Part 2 (Unit 3 & Review 1)
grace|noun|優美；優雅|The cat leapt onto the windowsill with grace, landing softly without a sound.|貓優雅地跳上窗台，沒有發出一絲聲音輕巧落下。
desperately|adv.|拚命地|The mother desperately searched the crowded mall for her missing child.|那位母親在擁擠的商場裡拚命尋找她走失的孩子。
reluctantly|adv.|勉強地；不情願地|John reluctantly agreed to tidy his bedroom after his mother had asked him for the fourth time.|John在媽媽第四次要求後，不情願地答應整理他的臥室。
describe|verb|描述|People often describe Zoe as someone who is dedicated to her work and never gives up.|人們經常描述Zoe是個致力於工作且永不放棄的人。
shiver|verb|顫抖|Jamie shivered with fear as the large dog drew nearer, but she knew she had to be brave.|當大狗靠近時，Jamie因害怕而發抖，但她知道自己必須勇敢。
faint|verb|昏厥；暈倒|After Ben ran for an hour in the heat, he was exhausted and nearly fainted.|在炎熱的天氣中跑了一個小時後，Ben筋疲力盡，幾近昏厥。
scientific|adj.|科學的|The students conducted a scientific experiment to find out how different light sources affect plant growth.|學生們進行了一項科學實驗，來了解不同光源如何影響植物生長。
frustration|noun|懊惱；沮喪|Harry yelled out in frustration when he realized he had locked himself out of the house.|當Harry發現他把自己鎖在屋外時，懊惱地大喊。
stubborn|adj.|頑固的；倔強的|Despite his friends' advice, Jack remained stubborn and refused to change his mind.|儘管朋友們勸告，Jack依然固執，拒絕改變心意。
rage|noun|暴怒；狂怒|Kris stormed out of the room in a rage after an argument with his brother.|Kris和兄弟爭吵後怒氣沖沖地走出房間。
rage|noun|暴怒；狂怒|Rita trembled with rage when she read the hurtful comments under her YouTube video.|Rita在讀到她YouTube影片下的惡意評論時，氣得發抖。
concentration|noun|專注|Julia began to lose concentration after she studied for several hours, so she decided to take a break.|Julia讀了幾個小時的書後，開始無法集中注意力，因此決定休息一下。
explain|verb|解釋|Before the first game started, the coach carefully explained the rules of the competition to her eager players.|在第一場比賽開始之前，教練仔細地向她熱切的球員們解釋比賽規則。
explain|verb|解釋|If you have any questions about the topic, I'll be happy to explain.|如果你對這個主題有任何疑問，我很樂意解釋。
sincerity|noun|真誠|Because George had said sorry with so much sincerity, Amy decided to forgive him.|由於George非常真誠地道歉，Amy決定原諒他。
confess|verb|坦白；認罪|Although stealing the car was his friend's idea, Kevin confessed that he was the one who actually drove it.|雖然偷車是朋友的主意，但Kevin坦承他是實際開車的人。
confess|verb|坦白；認罪|After Richard broke the neighbor's window, he felt guilty and decided to confess to his parents.|Richard打破鄰居的窗戶後，感到內疚，決定向父母坦白。
restore|verb|使恢復；使復原|After a long battle with illness, Lisa is now fully restored to health and enjoying life again.|在長期對抗病魔後，Lisa現在已完全恢復健康，重新享受生活。
fade|verb|逐漸消失|Mark's dream of becoming a writer started to fade when his first novel sold poorly.|Mark成為作家的夢想在他的第一本小說銷售不佳後開始消退。
hardly|adv.|幾乎不|Noah had no idea that Tammy sang so well. He could hardly believe it when he heard her beautiful voice.|Noah完全不知道Tammy唱得這麼好，當他聽到她美妙的歌聲時，幾乎不敢相信。
trace|noun|痕跡|The search team found no clues to the missing hiker; she had vanished without a trace.|搜救隊沒有找到那位失蹤登山客的任何線索，她似乎毫無痕跡地消失了。
trace|noun|痕跡|Despite their efforts, the police found no trace of the stolen jewelry.|儘管警方努力搜索，還是沒能找到被盜珠寶的任何痕跡。
depart|verb|去世；離開|The soldier departed this world peacefully after years of service to his country.|這位士兵在為國多年服務後，平靜地離世。
regret|noun|遺憾；懊悔|After his mother died, Henry expressed deep regret at not spending more time with her when she was alive.|母親過世後，Henry深感遺憾沒有在她生前多陪伴她。
regret|noun|遺憾；懊悔|Even though he got rejected, Bruce had no regrets about asking Jasmine out on a date.|儘管被拒絕了，Bruce並不後悔邀請Jasmine約會。
put aside|phrase|擱置|When her baby started crying, Maria put aside her dinner preparation to see what was wrong.|當她的寶寶開始哭時，Maria暫停準備晚餐去看出了什麼事。
from then on|phrase|從那時起|After I failed the exam, I decided to take my studies more seriously from then on.|考試不及格後，我決定從那時起更加認真地對待學業。
all the more|phrase|更加；格外|When Julie learned that her friend could go on vacation with her, she became all the more excited.|當Julie得知她的朋友可以一起去度假時，她變得更加興奮。
fly into a rage|phrase|勃然大怒|James heard the news and flew into a rage, shouting loudly and throwing things around.|James聽到這個消息後勃然大怒，大吼大叫並摔東西。
for one thing|phrase|首先；一方面|My family loves this restaurant very much. For one thing, the food is always delicious. For another, the staff is friendly.|我的家人非常喜歡這家餐廳。一方面，這裡的食物總是很美味；另一方面，工作人員也很友善。
be/become filled with|phrase|充滿|During the festival, the streets are filled with colorful decorations and lively music.|節日期間，街道充滿了色彩繽紛的裝飾和熱鬧的音樂。
slip away|phrase|消逝；死去|After a long battle with cancer, Gerry quietly slipped away in the early hours of the morning.|在經過漫長的抗癌後，Gerry在凌晨時分安詳地離世。
disagree|verb|不同意|Sam disagreed with Angie on which movie they should see, so they decided to go bowling instead.|Sam和Angie對於該看哪一部電影意見不同，所以他們決定改去打保齡球。
delightful|adj.|令人愉快的|Steve was glad that the party was such a success. It was delightful to see everyone enjoying themselves so much!|Steve很高興宴會辦得很成功。看到每個人都很開心真是令人愉快。
typically|adv.|一般而言|Traffic lights typically have just three colors: red, yellow, and green.|交通號誌燈一般而言只有三個顏色：紅色、黃色和綠色。
affair|noun|活動|Emma wants to make her wedding a truly grand affair, so she has dedicated a lot of time to preparing for it.|Emma想要把她的婚禮辦成非常隆重的一場活動，所以她花了很多時間準備。
spread|verb|傳播|The rumor about the movie star's death spread rapidly all over the country.|關於這位電影明星死亡的謠言迅速傳遍全國。
host|verb|主辦|After greeting everyone and saying a few words of welcome, the host showed the guests to the dining room.|向大家問候並說幾句歡迎之詞後，這位主人帶著客人們到飯廳。
engagement|noun|（尤指正式的）約會|Pat really wanted to join us for dinner, but unfortunately she had a previous engagement that she couldn't cancel.|Pat真的很想和我們一起晚餐，但很不幸地她早就有一個不能取消的約會。
quality|noun|品質|I can assure you that this necklace is of the highest quality.|我可以向您保證這條項鍊品質是最好的。
exquisite|adj.|精緻的|This exquisite vase was made by a master potter from China in the sixteenth century.|這個精緻的花瓶是十六世紀由來自中國的一位陶藝大師所製作。
luxurious|adj.|奢華的|Joey bought a luxurious house near the beach. It has an outdoor swimming pool and four big bedrooms.|Joey買了海灘附近的一棟豪宅。它擁有戶外游泳池和四個大臥房。
characteristic|adj.|獨特的|Large farms are characteristic of the northern part of this country.|這個國家的北部就是以大農場為特色。
agriculture|noun|農業|In India, over 70% of rural households depend on agriculture.|在印度，超過百分之七十的鄉村家庭仰賴農業。
starving|adj.|非常飢餓的|After swimming in the lake for five hours, the young men were starving, and each of them ordered two pizzas.|在湖裡游了五個小時後，這些年輕男子非常飢餓，每個人都點了兩個披薩。
energy|noun|能量|All the machines in this factory are powered by wind energy.|這間工廠所有的機器都是用風力來驅動。
fundamental|adj.|重要的；基礎的|Healthy food and clean living conditions are fundamental to having a good life.|健康的食物和乾淨的生活環境是擁有美好生活的基礎。
restricted|adj.|受限的|Entrance to the temple is restricted to those who are dressed appropriately.|這座廟宇只限那些穿著得體的人才能進入。
hear of|phrase|聽說過|I have never heard of mammals that only have one eye.|我從沒聽說過只有一隻眼睛的哺乳類動物。
make it to|phrase|成功（做到或抵達……）|With only two miles to go, the hikers were sure they would make it back to the camp before dark.|只剩兩英里要走，這些健行者有把握他們在天黑前可以回到紮營處。
ask for|phrase|要求；請求|Lucas wrote an email to the school office to ask for information about the scholarship.|Lucas寫了一封電子郵件給學校單位詢問有關獎學金的資訊。
invite someone over|phrase|邀請某人到家裡|Amy invited Lee and his fiancée over for dinner to celebrate their engagement.|Amy邀請Lee和他的未婚妻過來共享晚餐以慶祝他們訂婚。
refer to|phrase|稱呼|Bill likes to be referred to as "Doctor Cooper" when he's at work.|Bill在工作時喜歡人家稱呼他為「Cooper」博士。
as...as possible|phrase|儘可能|In the event of a fire, get out of the building as quickly as possible and then call for help.|火災發生時，儘快地離開建築物並大聲呼救。
